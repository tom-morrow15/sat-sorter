# Strike Integration - Quick Start

## What Just Got Added

Your Sat Sorter app now has **automatic Strike transaction tracking**! Here's what you can do:

### ✅ Connect Your Strike Account
1. Log in to Sat Sorter with Nostr
2. Go to your **Budget** page
3. Click **"Connect Strike"** button
4. Paste your Strike API key
5. Done! You're connected

### ✅ Sync Your Spending
Once connected, click **"Sync Strike"** to import all your transactions:
- Bill pay transactions
- Merchant purchases
- Bitcoin spending
- Money transfers

### ✅ Automatic Categorization
The system automatically sorts transactions into your budget categories:
- 🍔 **Food**: Restaurants, groceries, coffee shops
- 🚗 **Transportation**: Gas, Uber, airlines
- 🏠 **Utilities**: Electric, internet, water
- 📱 **Subscriptions**: Netflix, Spotify, gym memberships
- 💰 **Savings**: Bitcoin purchases and crypto exchanges
- And more!

### ✅ No Duplicates
The system is smart about tracking:
- Won't re-import the same transaction twice
- Only imports completed transactions
- Only fetches new transactions after first sync

## How to Get Your Strike API Key

1. Go to [Strike.me](https://strike.me)
2. Log in to your account
3. Click **Settings → API** (or Developer Settings)
4. Create a new API token
5. Copy it and paste into Sat Sorter

## Features

- **Automatic tracking** - See where every sat goes
- **Smart categories** - Transactions auto-sort into your buckets
- **No manual entry** - No more tracking spending by hand
- **Private** - Everything stays on your device
- **Manual control** - You decide what gets synced and when

## What Happens After Import

Once transactions are imported, you can:

1. **Move them around** - Reassign to different categories
2. **Edit them** - Change descriptions or amounts
3. **Delete them** - Remove unwanted transactions
4. **Track spending** - See how you're doing vs your budget

## Privacy Notice

- Your API key is stored **locally in your browser**
- No data is sent to any server
- The integration only **reads** transaction history
- You can disconnect anytime
